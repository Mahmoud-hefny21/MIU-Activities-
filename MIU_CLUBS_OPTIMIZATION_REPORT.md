The direct asset comparison is repeatable and controlled by choosing the same source files: 12 local homepage club photos total 841,410 bytes as originals; their 480-pixel derivatives total 316,389 bytes, a 525,021-byte (62.4%) reduction when those variants are served. Images load lazily and decode asynchronously.

No production bundle-size, field INP, controlled cold/warm load, server throughput, or site-wide navigation-speed claim was measured. The local Lighthouse captures found substantial main-thread work and variable layout shift; those remain performance follow-up areas.
